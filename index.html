The foundation

01Human flourishingBusiness is a tool for human flourishing. When your people are thriving, it is highly likely your business will thrive.

D · Your questions
20

Bonuses and the calendar

Question
Answer
When are Sprints and bonuses?
March through August, paid September 15. September through February, paid March 15.
Is my bonus subject to company profitability?
No. That caveat is removed. Bonuses are paid on time, as a percentage of your goal completion, on the 15th.
Why set goals in February and August, not month one?
Setting them in month one costs you a month of check-ins working on them.
D · Your questions
21

Your goals

Question
Answer
Can my goals change mid-Sprint?
Rarely, and only when a specific situation requires a pivot. Goals are not made up month to month.
Does my day-to-day performance count?
It is expected. If it is a point of emphasis for you, it becomes a goal or series of goals for improvement, measured objectively and placed in the right one of the three areas.
Who approves my goals?
Your supervisor refines them with you, then Dave or Darren give final approval.
D · Your questions
22

Who and how

Question
Answer
Who participates?
Everyone. This is how we mentor and coach. Independent brokers, including the Gast, Harpel, and Eli teams, opt in.
Who approves my completed Sprint?
Your supervisor confirms, then Dave or Darren sign off. Any issue is raised well in advance.
My supervisor travels. What about check-ins?
Phone or Teams counts. Check-ins sit on a fixed monthly date so travel does not make them slip.
Will the rules change again?
Not mid-Sprint. Any refinements are discussed before the next Sprint starts.

E · Our commitments
24

What is required of you

01Submit goals on timeDraft goals in all three areas by the February or August deadline.
02Get goals approvedRefine them with your supervisor. If Dave or Darren is not your supervisor, ask your supervisor to take your goals to them for final approval.
03Own your check-insMake sure it happens. Book it, send your 0 to 10 self-assessment ahead of time, and ask for coaching.
04Apply the coachingPut it to work every month, and ask your supervisor for help early when you hit an obstacle.

Why it matters

E · Our commitments
26

Supervisors commit to

01Take goal setting seriouslyHelp each direct report refine their goals every Sprint, and make sure they are challenging and bring real value to the company.
02Calibrate the scoresRead each self-assessment objectively, and adjust gently when a score looks too high or too low.
03Coach for clarityEvery month, give coaching on each goal, including what you want done differently.
04Close with clarityAt the Sprint review, explain clearly how each goal was or was not achieved.
05Carry it forwardTake the goal completion percentage and context to Dave or Darren for final bonus approval, so the bonus is paid on time.
